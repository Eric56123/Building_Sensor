## 3. Measured rig properties

### Modal frequencies

Three modes, each confirmed by **two independent methods**. Values are
bias-corrected by −0.245% (see §4).

| Mode | Sweep (PSD) | Ringdown | Recorded | ζ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3.00 Hz | 2.944 ± 0.003 Hz (5 taps) | **2.937 Hz** | 6.84% ± 0.40% |
| 2 | 8.00 Hz | 8.100 Hz (5 taps) | **8.080 Hz** | 0.70% ± 0.15% |
| 3 | 12.10 Hz | 12.190 Hz (7 taps) | **12.160 Hz** | not resolved |

**Mode identification.** Ratios 1 : 2.751 : 4.141 against uniform 4-DOF
shear-frame theory 1 : 2.879 : 4.412 (−4.4%, −6.1%). Three frequencies in
near-theoretical ratio identify them as modes 1–3 of one structure. This
resolved a genuine ambiguity: mode 1's damping (6.84%) is 10× mode 2's and on
its own suggested a mounting rather than structural mode. The three-mode series
settled it — under the alternative reading, f₂ would have to be ~23 Hz and both
2.94 and 12.19 Hz would be unexplained.

Mode 4 predicted ≈ 15 Hz, **not measurable** (above the shaker ceiling).

### Damping

ζ₁ = **6.84% ± 0.40%** (2 taps), ζ₂ = **0.70% ± 0.15%** (3 taps).
Log-decrement, least-squares fit through all peak amplitudes; each tap's two
independent estimators (log-decrement and envelope fit) agreed within 1%.

Mode 1's high damping is consistent with it generating the largest base shear
and so engaging base/mounting friction hardest.

### Noise floor (shaker off, 60 s)

| | As recorded | Scale-corrected |
|---|---|---|
| Broadband RMS | 10.24 mg | 12.54 mg |
| **In-band (0.5–45 Hz)** | **3.30 mg** | **4.04 mg** |

87% of noise energy lies above 55 Hz and is removed by the training bandpass.
Mains pickup (49–51 Hz) negligible at 0.334%. Two out-of-band peaks at
355/358 Hz, above the training band.

**SNR at the drive levels used: 30.7 dB in-band.**

### Linearity

**LINEAR** over a 2.2× drive range (0.325 → 0.722 g RMS), 3 repeats per gain.

```
1.4V:  mean f1 = 2.884 Hz   sd 0.0658
2.2V:  mean f1 = 3.017 Hz   sd 0.0475
2.8V:  mean f1 = 2.856 Hz   sd 0.0381
pooled within-gain sd = 0.0518 Hz (1.79%), 6 dof
t = -0.63, p = 0.573
```

Group means are **non-monotonic** — the middle gain is highest — which is
scatter, not trend.

**This is a bounded result, not proof of linearity:** the test can only detect
shifts above **3.59%**. A tighter bound would need ringdown at varied tap
amplitudes (ringdown resolves f₁ to 0.15% versus the sweep's 1.79%).

Adequate for the intended use: a loosened-screw damage signal is expected to
exceed 3.59%.

## 4. Known systematics

| Systematic | Magnitude | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency bias | **+0.245%** (all reported frequencies read high) | corrected in recorded values |
| Sensor sensitivity | **−18.3%** (all amplitudes read low) | **NOT corrected** — see below |

**Frequency bias.** True ODR is 1596.09 Hz, not the nominal 1600, so
`collect_window`'s 1000/1600 resample yields 997.56 Hz while `welch` is told
1000 Hz. Two independent 15 s and 20 s drains agreed to 0.004%. Not corrected in
code (it would require an awkward resample ratio); corrected arithmetically in
all recorded frequencies.

**Sensor sensitivity.** Measured 211 LSB/g against the datasheet's 256 — 18.3%
low, with FULL_RES set, offset trims at zero and DEVID reading 0xE5, i.e.
correctly configured and genuinely out of spec (consistent with a clone part).
Verified identical at ±2 g and ±16 g. **`config.ADXL345_SCALE` is still 0.0039**,
so every amplitude recorded this session — including the noise floor — is 82% of
true. Frequencies are unaffected, so all headline results stand.

## 5. Anomalies and discarded data

- **`REJECTED_sweep_1v4_r3_20260721_181529_raw.csv`** — discarded. Frequency
  progression chaotic, reaching 18–20 Hz (above the 15 Hz sweep limit); RMS 38%
  below its group siblings. Generator was likely being adjusted mid-capture.
  Re-run as a fresh r3. File retained.

- **FIFO overruns.** The ADXL345 sits on a bit-banged I²C bus with only 1.31×
  headroom over the ODR (76% duty cycle), so scheduling preemption causes
  intermittent overruns (~2 per 24 s). Each drops ≤32 samples (~20 ms).
  Sample spacing within surviving stretches is exact, so **peak frequencies are
  unaffected**; PSD magnitudes and noise floors are not. Mitigated with
  `--max-overruns 0` where magnitudes mattered (noise floor), accepted elsewhere.
  **Recommended fix before the damage campaign: move the ADXL345 to hardware I²C
  bus 1** (currently empty, 400 kHz).

- **DS3231 RTC absent.** Nothing responds on I²C bus 1. `monitor.py` is silently
  falling back to the Pi system clock for all timestamps.

- **Shaker ceiling ≈ 12 Hz.** With the generator verified sweeping to 100 Hz,
  response falls to the noise floor above ~12 Hz — the table stops moving. This
  is an amplifier or shaker limit, not a generator one, and is well below the
  APS 145's rating, so it may be recoverable (amplifier current limit is the
  first thing to check).

- **First sweep attempts** were logarithmic, not linear (deduced from the data:
  log fit R² = 0.9976 vs linear 0.9455). The envelope estimator inverts a linear
  law, so its estimates on those captures were invalid; PSD estimates were not.
  Later captures used a confirmed linear sweep.

## 6. Configuration changed during the session

| Change | Reason |
|---|---|
| `RECORDED_AXIS` z → **y** | z was the vertical (gravity) axis — the rig had been logging gravity, not horizontal excitation |
| `G_RANGE` ±2 g → **±16 g** | ±2 g clipped at both resonances (109 and 203 samples at the rail). FULL_RES holds scale constant across ranges — verified 211.1 vs 211.0 LSB/g — so the headroom is free |
| `BW_RATE` hardcoded → derived from `ODR_HZ` | changing `ODR_HZ` alone had left the sensor at 1600 Hz while the code resampled as though it were the new rate |

## 7. Verified empirically vs assumed

**Verified on this hardware:** ODR and effective sample rate; sensor sensitivity;
axis orientation (gravity + shake test); noise floor and its spectral
distribution; all three modal frequencies (two methods each); ζ₁ and ζ₂;
linearity with measured detection floor; absence of clipping; that the max-gain
sweep caused no structural change (pre/post taps agree to 0.12% on modes 2–3).

**Assumed, not verified:** that the 2.879/4.412 uniform shear-frame ratios are
the right theoretical comparison (the frame's mass/stiffness uniformity was not
measured); that mode 4 lies near 15 Hz (extrapolated, unreachable); that the
±16 g range change does not affect noise (scale was verified, noise was not
re-measured at the new range).

**Not measured:** function generator Vpp; amplifier gain in dB; amplifier
current limit; mode shapes (single sensor); f₄.
